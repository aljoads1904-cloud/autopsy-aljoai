L01

PO Intake Chaos

"How many emailed POs do you process per week manually, and what's the error rate?"

Finding: Blackwood receives an average of 38 POs per week across 6 different formats — PDFs, Excel, Slack DMs, even a photo of a handwritten note. Senior ops manager spends 3 hours per Monday on intake. Error rate sits at 8%, costing $18,300 annually in shipping credits alone, plus margin loss on misshipped product.

Reorder Friction

"Do your wholesale customers have a self-serve portal to reorder, or is every order initiated manually?"

Finding: No portal. Every reorder begins with a buyer email. Average reorder cycle is 38 days — 8 days longer than the cafe's actual inventory cycle suggests. Cafes report "I forget to reorder until I'm running low" in roughly 30% of accounts. Mike has built workarounds in his head, but the system itself creates the friction.
